Welcome to Klara Wisper

Meeting transcripts and dictation in Hebrew, right on your Mac. No audio leaves the computer.

What happens now:

  1. Click Continue and Install, and approve with your Mac's password (or Touch ID).
  2. When it's done, Klara Wisper opens by itself with a short guide: it checks the Mac, asks two questions, picks the version that fits, downloads the transcription engine, connects to Claude and checks that everything works.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 14.4 or later, about 6 GB free and an internet connection the first time.
